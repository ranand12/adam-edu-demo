## Slide 2: Content & Research — Analyze, Search, and Create

> **Time:** ~3 minutes (overview); expand during demos

### AI Assistant — File Analysis
"This is your secure research sandbox. Drop a CSV, a PDF, a paper — Gemini reads it and finds the story your data is trying to tell. No code, no cleanup.

In the demo, I'll upload an RNA-seq dataset and a CRISPR paper. Gemini identifies the top differentially expressed genes, explains the biology, flags potential batch effects — and then cross-references the paper to suggest which genes are the best candidates for a knockout study. All in one conversation. Data stays within your Google Workspace — it never leaves your tenant."

### Grounded Web Search
"What used to take two hours of PubMed tabs now takes one prompt. Ask a research question — Gemini searches the live web, not its training data — and comes back with cited answers you can verify.

I'll show a faculty member preparing an NSF CAREER proposal: we'll find recent publications, active funding opportunities, and even SUNY-specific research across the 64-campus system. Every fact is linked to a source. This replaces the first two hours of a literature review, not the whole thing — but those are the most tedious two hours."

### Media Generation
"Turn a text prompt into publication-ready visuals. Workflow diagrams, research posters, infographics, presentation graphics — no Photoshop required.

Think of these as first drafts for your visuals. A PhD student preparing a conference poster can get a layout, a workflow diagram for their CRISPR experiment, and a proper figure caption — all from prompts. Great for lab meeting slides, departmental presentations, and grant figure concepts."

## Slide 3: Knowledge & Discovery — Synthesize, Discover, and Explore

> **Time:** ~3 minutes (overview); expand during demos

### Research Knowledge Agents (Gems)
"This is where it gets powerful for PIs. You can train a custom AI assistant — called a Gem — that knows your lab's protocols, your datasets, your terminology. Then share it with your entire team.

I'll create one live: a Lab Methods Assistant for a molecular biology lab. It knows the Western blot protocol, the CRISPR transfection steps, the biosafety rules, even the equipment booking process. No code required — if you can write an email, you can create an agent. You write protocols once, your entire team benefits, and the answers stay consistent. No more telephone game across lab members."

### NotebookLM Literature Review
"Three papers, one notebook, zero hallucinations — and a podcast for your commute.

Upload your papers and NotebookLM becomes your co-reader. It summarizes, cross-references, and synthesizes across ALL your uploaded sources simultaneously. Every claim is cited with inline references — click to jump to the source page. It will NOT hallucinate facts from the internet because it only uses YOUR documents.

The killer feature: Audio Overview. Two AI hosts discuss your papers in a podcast-style format. Listen while commuting. Great for committee meeting prep. You can add up to 300 sources per notebook — an entire literature corpus."

### Deep Research
"A 30-page lit review while you grab lunch — with every source cited.

Point Gemini at a research question and it autonomously searches, reads, and synthesizes dozens to hundreds of web sources into a comprehensive report with footnotes. It shows you its research plan first — you can review and modify it before it starts.

I'll show two examples: a competitive analysis benchmarking SUNY's AI computing infrastructure against peer institutions, and a comprehensive literature review on graph neural networks in drug discovery. Tasks that would take a human 4–8 hours, done in minutes. Always verify key claims — it's a first draft, not the final word."
